# Section titles

A pipeline heading over a section of a page, card or panel is `SectionTitle` (`components/section-title.tsx`), not a hand-styled `h2`/`h3`. `size="sm"` (the default) is a part of a card or panel, `size="lg"` a section of a page or drawer; `as="h2"` sets the level without changing the look.

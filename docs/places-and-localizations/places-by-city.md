# Places listed by city or country

When the photo has a city (its custom **City**, or else its EXIF one), the place comboboxes list only the places in that city; with no city but a country, only the places in that country. A place is in a city or country when it has it, or the nearest place it's in has it, and names compare by their [other names](localizations.md#other-names) ignoring case, so a place in “USA” is listed for a photo in “United States” once “USA” is one of its other names. Places with no city or country, and inferred places, aren't listed. The place a row has chosen always stays in its list. A line above the rows says “Listing places in Seoul.” with **Show all places**, which lists every place until **Only matching places**; it's not saved. Choosing a place with a city fills in the photo's city, so the second row lists that city's places. A photo with neither lists every place. When the second (less specific) place is chosen, the first row lists only the places in it, at any depth, such as Geunjeongjeon and Geunjeongmun Gate for Gyeongbokgung Palace, and the line adds “places in Gyeongbokgung Palace for the first place”. **Show all places** lifts both.

> **Frontend analogy:** like a cascading select, where picking a state narrows the city dropdown, with an escape hatch to search everything.

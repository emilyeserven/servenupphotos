# Photo book captions

Each photo's caption is filled in from its data with the book's caption template, the same templates and placeholders the Photo page's **Generate** uses for social media posts, including changes not committed yet. The [camera and lens overrides](../camera-overrides.md) and the photo's custom lens are made over its data first, as **Apply overrides** would, so a caption's camera, lens, focal length and aperture are the overridden ones even for a photo whose file doesn't have them applied yet. With no template picked, the book uses the first template for the **Photo Book** platform, or else the first one for any platform. A template's [smaller and italic text](../caption-templates.md#smaller-and-italic-text) is drawn that way here and in the PDF. Typing in a caption keeps what's typed instead, and **From template** under it goes back to the template's. Captions are filled in from the template and the photos' data each time they're drawn, so a change saved in the same tab shows right away. **Refresh all captions**, on the **Info** tab, is always there: it reads the changes waiting for Commit from the backend again, so a template or photo saved in another tab or window reaches the captions too, and, after asking, does what **From template** does for every caption typed over at once. A caption on an empty place keeps what's typed, since there's no photo to fill it in from.

## Caption titles

A caption's first line is the photo's title, drawn bold and larger, with the lines under it small. A caption can also have no title, and then every line is drawn alike as small caption text, on the pages, in Preview Mode, in the PDF and in **Save as image**, which all draw the same page. A caption filled in from the template has no title when:

- its photo has no title, so the template's first line with text (`{title}` in the starter template) comes out empty, and the line under it is no longer drawn as a title;
- its first line, marked **Title** under [Hiding places](#hiding-places), is hidden for that photo;
- **Caption titles** is off on the **Info** tab, which leaves every caption's title line out of the book.

A caption with no title is kept with a blank first line, so a caption typed over has no title when its first line is blank, and typing over a caption with no title starts with that blank line (`apps/client-pipeline/src/lib/photo-book-caption-titles.ts`).

> **Frontend analogy:** the blank first line works like a flag stored in the text itself, the way a leading `---` marks front matter in a Markdown file: everything that draws a caption reads the same string, so no extra prop has to reach each one.

## Captions that overflow

A caption whose text runs past the room its layout gives it (above or below), or off the page's printed area, is outlined in red with **Caption overflows** beside it, on Photo Pages and in the Book tab's preview; neither shows in Preview Mode or the PDF. Under the Book tab's preview, a list names each overflowing caption in the book by page and photo, and clicking one shows its page. A caption is measured whenever it or its page changes size or its text changes, so it's listed once its page has been drawn, here or on Photo Pages, and leaves the list as soon as it fits. Captions are sized in shares of the page's width, so a small preview measures the same as a full page (`apps/client-pipeline/src/lib/caption-overflow.ts`).

## Hiding places

**Hide places**, on Photo Pages left of a page or spread's tags, lists each of its photos with the parts of its location: each of its places (or its place name when it has none), its city, its state and its country. Clicking one leaves it out of that photo's caption in this book only, as if the photo didn't have it, to keep a long place name short where there's little room. Everything that reads the location fills in without it: `{places}`, the numbered places (a hidden 1st place makes the 2nd the 1st), `{city}` and the rest, and the [place name builder](../caption-templates.md#place-name-builder)'s forms and conditions, so a form that needs a state no longer fits once the state is hidden. Under them are the lines of the photo's caption as the template fills it in, each by its text; clicking one leaves the whole line out, such as a romanized place line or the camera line for one photo. The first line, when it's the photo's title, is marked **Title** in bold, and leaving it out draws the lines left without a title (see [Caption titles](#caption-titles)). A line is known by where it is in the caption (the 1st, the 2nd, …), counted after hidden places have dropped any line left empty, so hiding a place that empties a line above a hidden one shifts which line is hidden. The button says how many places and lines are hidden on the page or spread. The photo's own data doesn't change, and a caption typed over shows the change once **From template** puts the template's back (`apps/client-pipeline/src/lib/photo-book-hidden.ts`).

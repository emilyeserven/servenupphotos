# Places and Localizations

`/places` and `/localizations`, under **Configuration** in the sidebar, keep two more kinds of data next to the photos, albums and tags. The portfolio shows each photo's places, and writes places, cities and countries the way its **Script display** setting on the portfolio's Settings page says (see [On the portfolio](places-and-localizations/on-the-portfolio.md)).

# Camera filters

The Grid's filters panel also has a **Gear & settings** button after **When** that opens one picker for the camera filters: the gear a photo was taken with and how it was shot. Its left side lists **Cameras**, **Lenses**, **Focal length**, **Aperture**, **Exposure** and **ISO**, each with what it's set to under its name ("Any" while it isn't) and a dot while it's set; the right side shows the chosen one's choices. Unlike **When**, any number of them can be set at once, and the button's badge counts how many are. The picker opens on the first one that's set. It keeps 16px from the screen's edges. On phones the list folds up above the choices, behind a button naming the chosen one, and choosing one folds it up again.

**Cameras** and **Lenses** list every camera or lens (`?camera=Fujifilm X-T5`, `?lens=EF50mm f/1.8 II`; a photo whose EXIF didn't name a lens is under none) with how many photos each would show; **All cameras** or **All lenses** comes first, and clicking the checked one again goes back to all. A **Group by** button in the title row, beside the **Sort** button and left of the **(i)**, opens a menu that picks how it's laid out: **None**, every one once, A to Z (the default); **Brand**; or **Type**. Brand and Type are the top-level metadata groups with those names (that group's own row left out, what's under it a level up, then any camera or lens it doesn't list). Cameras without such a group get their own, guessed from the model name: Brand, such as Canon or Fujifilm, and Type (Mirrorless, DSLR, Compact or Phone), each under a heading, with Unknown last. A grouping with nothing in it isn't offered, and the list opens on the grouping of a chosen group. Each button is shaded while it isn't on its default. **Sort** opens a menu of the order: **Name** keeps the grouping's order, and **Oldest first** or **Newest first** orders the cameras or lenses under each heading or group by when the first of the view's photos was taken with each. A choice that would show no photos given the other filters is left out, unless chosen; **Show N choices hidden by the other filters** lists them dimmed, and hovering one says which filters hide it: each one that, cleared alone, would let it show photos ("Hidden by Aperture or Lens"), or "the other filters together" when no single one would. The **(i)** button across from the **Cameras** or **Lenses** title opens the picker's info pane (see [Picker conventions](clearing-filters.md#picker-conventions)), the same one the settings' graphics use, with the details of the camera or lens pointed at, or else the chosen one: its own sized picture (exactly 267 × 200) and description from the pipeline's [Gear Pictures](../gear-pictures.md) page, or else a drawing of its kind (a DSLR, mirrorless, compact or phone, guessed from its model name) (for a lens, a side view of it, longer for a longer focal length and with a zoom ring when it zooms). A camera then lists its kind, how many photos used it, and from when to when. A lens lists its focal length, whether it's a prime or zoom, and its widest aperture, read from its name, and how many photos used it, on which cameras, at which focal length most, and from when to when. A group, such as a brand, has no details. The four camera settings each have a slider with a handle at each end, and their quick picks listed under it. A slider steps through the values the view's photos have before any filter (the portfolio's photos, or the Gallery's own in the pipeline), lowest first, so every step changes what's shown, and says the range it's set to, such as "f/4 – f/5.6". The URL changes once a handle is let go, keeping the range's ends as the photos write them (`?iso=100-400`, `?exposure=1/500-1/30`, `?focalLength=18-50`, `?aperture=4-5.6`); dragging both handles back to the ends clears it. Each setting's title sits above its slider, which has a tick under it for every step and its lowest and highest values at its ends. The **(i)** button across from the title opens the info pane with little graphics of what its two ends look like, following the handles as they're dragged: the angle of view for focal length, the opening for aperture beside a person in front of trees whose blur grows as the f-number drops, a dial filled for longer exposures beside a moving ball that's sharp at a fast shutter and smeared into a streak at a slow one, and grain for ISO, with a sentence on what the setting does. Clicking it again closes it; the browser remembers which, for every setting. A photo without the setting isn't in any range.

The hours of the day are in the [When](when.md) picker, which every view with filters has.

**Orientation** is a section of the **Visuals** picker (see [Portfolio Color](../portfolio-color.md#on-the-grid-page)): a list of **Horizontal**, **Vertical** and **Square**, each drawn as an outlined shape of its proportions, with how many photos the other filters (colors too) leave have that orientation. Clicking one shows only the photos wider than they're tall (`?orientation=horizontal`), taller than they're wide (`?orientation=vertical`), or exactly as wide as they're tall (`?orientation=square`); clicking it again clears it. The photo's size is the one its JSON file saves (`width` and `height`), so a photo not uploaded to R2 yet has no orientation.

The other views don't have these filters, and the menu's links leave them out. They count toward the button's number, and the button's clear X clears them too.

> **Frontend analogy:** the slider's value is an index into a sorted array of the values, not the value itself, so `1/4000` and `30` seconds sit one step apart when nothing's between them, like `<input type="range">` over `values[i]`.

## Metadata groups

The [metadata groups](../metadata-groups.md) in `metainfo/METAINFO.json` show up in the Grid's filters, in their order (lowest `order` first, then by name, each group under the one it's in):

- **Camera** and **Lens** list their groups first, each indented under the group it's in and followed by the cameras or lenses it lists, then every camera or lens no group lists. Choosing a group (`?lensGroup=lens-zoom`, `?cameraGroup=<id>`) shows the photos taken with any value it or a group inside it lists; choosing a group clears a chosen name and the other way round. A group that would show no photos, given the other filters, isn't listed, unless it's the one chosen.
- **ISO**, **Exposure**, **Focal length** and **Aperture** each list a quick pick for each of their groups under the slider. Each pick shows the lowest and highest values the photos actually have inside the group's range, not the range as it's set: a **Moderate** ISO group of 201 to 1600 shows **250 – 1600 ISO** when those are the photos' nearest values, and Wide angle's 10 to 24 mm shows **11mm – 24mm** when no photo was taken at 10 mm. Pointing at one (or tabbing to it) previews it: the handles, the range written above them and the info pane's graphics move to its values until the pointer leaves, without changing the URL. Choosing one moves the handles to those values (`?focalLength=11-24`); the list checks the one the slider is set to, and choosing that one again clears the slider. A group's range runs from the lowest to the highest end of its own range, its values and those of every group in it; a range with only a `min` runs up from it, and one with only a `max` up to it. A group no photo is in isn't listed.

> **Frontend analogy:** a quick pick is a preset that calls the same `onChange` as letting go of the handles, so the URL only ever holds the plain range; the list works out which one to check by comparing its range to the URL's. Pointing at one is like a hover preview: the picker keeps the pointed-at range in local state and the slider and graphics read it ahead of the URL's, the same way they follow a handle while it's dragged.

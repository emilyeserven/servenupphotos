# Counts

Each choice says how many photos it would show in that view, given the other filters, so a place's count is out of the photos the chosen album and tag (and, on the Grid, camera, lens and ranges) leave. The Map counts only photos it can place and the Grid only photos with resized versions.

When the filters leave no photos, the view says so instead of its list; the Map shows no markers and moves out to the whole world. Changing a filter on the Map fits it to the photos it now shows.

A choice that would show no photos, given the other filters, isn't listed, unless it's the one chosen (or, in **Places**, has a chosen one inside it). The dropdown ends with a greyed-out line saying how many were hidden, and the Places columns a note under them, such as "40 places hidden by the other filters".

# Trip Stops

Trip stops were called places visited; they're named apart from the [saved places](../places-and-localizations.md) on the Places page, which a stop can be added from. An old `/trip-lines/<day>/places` link opens Trip Stops. In the files they're still `visits`.

The day's **Trip Stops** page lists the stops along the day's route, with or without a photo: a restaurant, café, bar, shop, museum, sight, station or other. It's only for adding them: **Add a stop on the map** drops one where the map is clicked; **Add a saved place nearby** adds one of the [saved places](../places-and-localizations.md) within about 10 km of the day's photos, named after it; and **Add at this coordinate** adds one at a latitude and longitude typed or pasted in, such as `34.6672, 135.5013` from Google Maps (degrees and minutes work too); and **Add a station** finds a train, subway, bus or ferry station by name near the day's first photo (within about 100 km on Photon) (the [station search](../trips.md) a Trip's legs use) and adds it as a **Station** stop, such as one changed trains at. Each row says where the stop is on the route, and its **Where on the route** menu sets it: not on the route, before photo 1, or after any photo (the photo strip on Route sets the same). Each has a kind, a name (needed to save), a note, and a link to it on Google Maps, Naver Map or any other map, which opens from the row. The map shows each as a round pin with its kind's icon.

A place visited twice, such as a station gone back to by car, gets **Visited again** on its row: a copy (the same name, kind, spot, note and link, but not the first visit's casual photos) off the route, whose **Where on the route** then puts it elsewhere.

A stop on the route also has **Got here by**: how it was reached from the point before it, such as **Walked** between two stations in the middle of a train ride, or **Car or taxi** back to a station in the middle of a walk. Left at **The route's way**, it's travelled as the gap it's in. Otherwise the stop is saved with `"mode"` and the route is cut there: the stretch into the stop is its own segment in that mode (from the photo or stop before it, with no photo of its own when between two stops), and the line goes on from the stop the gap's way. The photo strip shows the way as a small icon before the stop's chip. The gaps read back from a cut line are the same, since the stretch from a gap's last stop to its photo keeps the gap's way (`lib/stop-ways.ts`). A train, subway, bus or transit hop is also cut at every trip stop on it, even one reached the route's way, so each stretch is its own row under [Lines ridden](lines-ridden.md).

## Localized names

A stop can have **localized names**, the same three a [saved place's localization](../places-and-localizations.md) has: its **Romanization** (Ichiran Dōtonbori-ten), **Local pronunciation** (いちらん どうとんぼりてん) and **Local script** (一蘭 道頓堀店). The translate button at the end of its row opens them under it, already open when any is written. On a stop added from a saved place, each one left empty shows the place's localization faintly, and the portfolio uses the place's for it, so a saved place's names needn't be typed again. They're saved on the stop as `"romanization"`, `"pronunciation"` and `"localScript"`, trimmed, each up to 200 characters, and left out when empty. **Visited again** copies them. The day's [Trip page](../trip-pages.md#trip-stops) shows them.

> **Frontend analogy:** like `placeholder` versus `value` on an input: the saved place's localization is the placeholder, and anything typed on the stop is the value that wins.

## Casual photos

A stop can have **casual photos**: snapshots taken there, such as the food at a restaurant, that show only with the stop on the day's [Trip page](../trip-pages.md#trip-stops), never in the Gallery, the portfolio's Grid or Map, or on a trip line. **Add casual photos** under a stop opens a grid of every photo taken that day (on the portfolio, drafts, Undecided ones and casual photos of other stops; `components/trip-lines/casual-photos.tsx`), and picking one adds it. Its thumbnail's cross takes it off again. The stop keeps their IDs in `photos` (at most 50, each once), and each photo's file gets `"casual": true` as a local edit waiting for Commit (see [Drafts](../drafts.md)); taking a photo off, or removing the stop, clears the mark. **Save line** so the stop keeps its photos.

A casual photo is left off the day's photo strip and line, out of `ALLPHOTOS.json`, and out of the Dashboard's **Drafts** card and **Needs attention** counts, since it needs no title, tags, album or GPS of its own.

> **Frontend analogy:** `casual` is like a `hidden` flag on a CMS entry: the record still exists and the editor can see it, but the public list query filters it out, and only the one page that asks for it by ID shows it.

They're saved with the line as `visits`:

```json
{
  "name": "Ichiran Dotonbori",
  "kind": "restaurant",
  "latitude": 34.6688,
  "longitude": 135.5026,
  "note": "Ramen before the river walk",
  "link": "https://maps.app.goo.gl/…",
  "place": "ichiran-dotonbori",
  "localScript": "一蘭 道頓堀店",
  "after": 305,
  "photos": [311, 312]
}
```

The photo strip on **Route** puts these places on the route too: the plus under each gap lists the day's places not on the route yet, and picking one puts it between those two photos (with a link to the Trip Stops page to add more). Below them it lists each place already on the route, marked **again**: picking one adds a second visit there, a copy as **Visited again** makes, leaving the first where it is, such as a ropeway's bottom station gone up from in the morning and back to after lunch (`lib/stop-insert.ts`). `after` is the photo it comes after. The line then goes from that photo through the place to the next one: the segment lists it as a via spot with `"visit": 0` (its index in `visits`), after the via spots of its own, so **Snap to streets** routes through it and the map draws it on the line. The photo strip shows the place's icon between those two photos, its name shown on hover. Before each place is the way it was reached from the point before it (its **Got here by**, or else the gap's), and after the last one is the gap's own way, to the next photo. Each changes on its own: picking **Ropeway or gondola** before the second of two stations makes just the stretch between them a ropeway, and changing the gap after them keeps the ways to them (a stop that went the gap's way is saved with that way first), so a car to a station and a walk from it stay a car and a walk. Between groups each way is a menu; in a break the places show no way. Pointing at that icon shows arrows that move the place one spot earlier or later on the route: first past the next stop between the same two photos, such as two stops before the first photo, then on one photo, landing next to the stops already there. A cross takes it off the route again. Moving one earlier from after the first photo, or picking it from the strip's **Start**, puts it before the first photo, as `"first": true` with no `after`, such as lunch before the first photo was taken: the line goes from the start spot through it to the first photo (as a via spot after the start spot), or, with no start spot, starts at it. The way from there to the first photo shows before photo 1 in the strip and can be changed like any gap, such as a bus from lunch: the line is then a hop from the start to the first photo before the walk, saved as the first segment with that mode, a `start` and only the first photo. Moving one later from the last gap, or picking it from the strip's **End**, puts it after the last photo, such as dinner after the last photo was taken: the line ends there. With an end spot, the line goes from the last photo through it to the end spot (as a via spot after the last photo); with none, the last segment's `end` is the place, named after it, and when the day ends with more than one such place, the line goes through the others to the last one added. The strip shows the way there and the place's icon after the last photo, and loading the line again keeps that `end` as the place rather than an end spot of its own. That way can be changed like any gap, such as a drive back to the station: the line is then a hop from the last photo to the end, saved as the last segment with that mode, only the last photo and an `end` (the draft's `endGap`). A place not on the route is a pin only, and a place put after a break in the middle of the day has no line to sit on, so it stays a pin too. The editor works the via spot out from `after` each time, so moving or removing the place keeps the line in step; the backend checks that `after` is one of the day's photos with an exact spot (not necessarily on a drawn segment, so a stop can follow a photo on an automatic line or at a break) and that every `visit` names a place the day has.

> **Frontend analogy:** the via spot is derived state, like a `useMemo` over `visits`: `visits` is the source of truth, and the saved via spot is only written out so the portfolio and the router don't need to know about places.

`place` is the saved place's ID when one was picked. The link has to be an `http` or `https` web address. Commit bakes each into `TRIPLINES.geo.json` as a GeoJSON `Point` with `"feature": "visit"`, the day, and the name, kind, note and link, next to the lines, and counts them (`tripLineVisits`). The Timeline's route sketches draw them; the portfolio's Map doesn't.
